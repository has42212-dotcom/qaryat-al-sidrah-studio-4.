1. Who we are

Qaryat Al-Sidrah Studio (the "App") is operated by Aya Askar ("we", "us"), who is responsible for the information described in this Policy. Contact: has42212@gmail.com.

2. What the App does

The App is a desktop video production studio for the Qaryat Al-Sidrah animated series. It runs on the operator's own computer, where it organises, edits, checks and exports videos. When a video is finished, the App can send it to a connected TikTok account as a draft. The account owner then reviews the draft in the TikTok app and decides whether to post it.

The App never publishes automatically. It does not use TikTok's Direct Post feature and does not request TikTok's publishing permission.

4. How TikTok sign-in works

  1. The App opens TikTok's official authorisation page (TikTok Login Kit for Desktop) in your browser. You sign in to TikTok there, so your password goes only to TikTok.
  2. TikTok shows the permissions requested (user.info.basic, video.upload), and you choose whether to allow them.
  3. The sign-in uses PKCE: a new one-time secret is created for every sign-in, and TikTok receives only its SHA-256 fingerprint. A random state value is checked to confirm the response belongs to the sign-in you started. Responses that fail this check are discarded.
  4. TikTok returns you to a temporary address on your own computer (http://localhost:3455/callback/). The App listens there only while a sign-in is in progress, for at most 10 minutes. It then exchanges the one-time code for tokens directly with TikTok over HTTPS.

5. How the information is used

The information is used only to:

  • connect your TikTok account and keep the connection working;
  • send a video to your TikTok account as a draft, when the operator starts an upload for that video;
  • check whether the draft was delivered to your TikTok inbox;
  • keep a record of uploads with the series' production files.

It is not used for advertising, profiling, selling, or training machine-learning models.

6. Where and how it is stored

  • Only on the operator's computer. TikTok tokens and your account identifier are saved in one file in the App's private credentials folder. The App's own TikTok client credentials, issued to the App by TikTok, are kept in the same folder. Nothing is sent to a server we operate, because there is none.
  • Excluded from version control. The credentials folder is excluded from the App's version control, so tokens and client credentials are not included in copies of the project made that way.
  • Never displayed or logged. The App does not print or log tokens, the client secret or authorisation codes, and its upload records contain none of them.
  • Encrypted in transit. All communication with TikTok uses HTTPS. Video data is sent only to the upload address TikTok issues for that upload, and the App refuses an upload address that is not HTTPS.
  • At rest, protected by the computer's user account. The token file is an ordinary file protected by the operating system's account permissions. The App does not add its own encryption.
  • Least privilege. The App asks TikTok only for user.info.basic and video.upload.

7. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your information. Information leaves the computer only:

  • to TikTok: sign-in and token requests, the video files approved for upload, status checks, and revocation when you disconnect. TikTok handles that data under its own Privacy Policy;
  • to authorities, if we are required to disclose it by law.

8. Retention

The App has no automatic time-based deletion. Each item is kept as follows:

On TikTok's side: drafts delivered to your inbox, anything you post, and TikTok's own records are held and controlled by TikTok and by you in the TikTok app. The App cannot delete anything from TikTok.

9. Disconnecting and deletion

  • Disconnect through the App: the operator runs the App's disconnect command. It asks TikTok to revoke the App's access (TikTok's documented revocation, after which the App no longer appears among your connected apps) and deletes the token file from the computer. If TikTok cannot be reached, the local file is still deleted, and you should then also remove the App in TikTok.
  • Disconnect in TikTok: you can remove the App at any time in your TikTok account's settings, in the section that lists apps connected to your account. The stored tokens stop working. To also delete the local token file, ask us (below).
  • Ask us: email has42212@gmail.com to have your tokens and upload records deleted. We will confirm when it is done, and we aim to respond within 30 days.
  • Disconnecting does not delete drafts or posts on TikTok. Manage or delete those in the TikTok app.

11. YouTube

The App can also be connected to the series' YouTube channel through Google's sign-in, which uses YouTube API Services. Today the App uses this connection only to read the connected channel's name and ID, to confirm it is the correct channel. The App does not currently upload videos to YouTube or change anything on the channel. We will update this Policy before adding YouTube uploads.

The App requests the YouTube permissions youtube.upload and youtube. Google's tokens are stored in the same local credentials folder, under the same protections as described in section 6. They are kept until the operator deletes them. To revoke access, use myaccount.google.com/permissions; the App has no YouTube disconnect command, so ask us to delete the local token file too. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
